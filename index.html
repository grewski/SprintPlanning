About this tool
Forecast with confidence, not certainty.

Use historic throughput or velocity to explore sprint and release outcomes. More data points usually produce a more useful model.

Results are statistical estimates, not delivery guarantees. Changing scope, team conditions and other real-world factors can affect outcomes.
